## Research integrity and use boundaries

1. Publisher-side requests do **not** prove model training, retrieval, citation, recommendation or downstream influence. The Five-Layer Framework separates them.
2. A historical proposition that an LLM will “always” recommend the least-resistant entity is **unvalidated** and must be interpreted only as an early hypothesis, not a universal empirical law.
3. User-Agent names and cached telemetry labels do not by themselves authenticate organizations.
4. DOI registration, GitHub publication, source availability and institutional correspondence **do not constitute** peer review, EU endorsement or certification.
5. The author has a commercial interest in ENTIA. The public notes include research limitations and do not license ENTIA's proprietary corpus, personal information or restricted evidence.
6. AI-assisted drafting is disclosed where relevant. Final claims and responsibility rest with the named author.
